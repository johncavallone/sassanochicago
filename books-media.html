The manuscript, sources, and published research behind this project.

Sassano Chicago: Chicago's Forgotten Italian Enclave

The full manuscript documenting the history of the Federal Street enclave — from the first arrivals in the 1890s through displacement in the 1950s — drawing on parish records, Cook County deeds, U.S. Census data, and oral histories collected from descendants.

Primary Sources

  • San Giovanni Evangelista Church parish records — baptisms, marriages, and deaths, Sassano, Italy, 1644–present
  • Sassano civil birth, marriage, and death records, 1809–present
  • Santa Maria Incoronata Church records, Chicago
  • Cook County death certificates and Torrens property records
  • U.S. Federal Census records, 1900–1950
