The Sassano Project

Peter Barbella

Civil Records

Pasquale Cirullo

In the fall of 2018, I came across Peter Barbella's "The Sassano Project" — a genealogical treasure. We've since become good friends, and he invited me to help index the records of San Giovanni Evangelista Church, dating back to 1644, along with Sassano's civil records going back to 1809, working alongside Pasquale Cirullo. Their work has made it possible for anyone with Sassano roots to trace their ancestors back, in some cases, as far as their ninth or tenth great-grandparents.
