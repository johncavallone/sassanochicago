The earliest surnames in Sassano.

Of the 48 documented surnames on this page, 47 already show up in individuals born in the 1600s — some as early as the 1590s. All of these are estimated ("ABT") birth years rather than exact-dated records, reconstructed backward from later baptism, marriage, and death entries, so treat them as approximate. Two of these entries are recorded under an earlier spelling that later evolved into the modern surname: Fornino appears from 1608 as Florendino, and Romanelli appears from 1620 as Romaniello — both names track the same family lines, with the older spelling gradually giving way to the newer one over the following century or two. One surname — Finizio — doesn't yet have an indexed individual born before 1700; that likely reflects gaps in what's been transcribed so far rather than the family arriving later.
