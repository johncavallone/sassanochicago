226 records · three Chicago parishes · 1893–1915

Chicago church baptisms & marriages index.

Santa Maria Incoronata, the Riciglianesi parish at Alexander and Wentworth, was adopted by the Sassanesi living several miles south — but it wasn't the only church they used. Assumption BVM, further north, was the parish of choice for the earliest arrivals, including Cono Stavola, likely the first Sassanese in Chicago. This index tracks Sassanesi (and a few related-town) baptisms, marriages, and deaths across all three.

Where the earliest Sassanesi worshipped.

Before Federal Street had a large enough Sassanese population to gravitate to Santa Maria Incoronata, the earliest arrivals — including Cono Stavola and Maria Policastro's 1893 marriage, described on the Home page as likely the first Sassanese couple in Chicago — used Assumption BVM.
