Federal Street Torrens.

Real estate tract records, warranty deeds, and probate filings for the properties Sassanesi families owned along Federal Street — transcribed ledger by ledger from the original Cook County Torrens documents.

Sassano Italians who settled in Chicago from 1893 until the late 1950s lived on Federal Street (formerly Armour Avenue). Because the properties along 4700–5100 S Federal Street were purchased by the Chicago Housing Authority to build the Robert Taylor Homes, the PINs (Permanent Index Numbers) for that real estate no longer exist — the tract, deed, and probate records below are what's left of the paper trail.

How to read this page: each tract is identified by Book–Page–Lot, e.g. Book 342, Page 328, Lot 13, 14. Only transactions involving Sassanesi Italians who owned real estate here have been transcribed — other owners' transactions in the same tract are not included. Where a document number is underlined like this, it links to the actual scanned document. Instrument codes: WD = Warranty Deed, QC = Quit Claim, TD = Trust Deed, R / REL = Release, NTC = Notice, INTY = Probate Inventory, AGMT = Agreement, BOND = Bond, D = Deed, CHG = Change, AFFT = Affidavit.
