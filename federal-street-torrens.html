4700–5100 S Federal Street · Tract Pages

Federal Street Torrens.

Real estate tract records and warranty deeds for the properties Sassanesi families owned along Federal Street — transcribed ledger by ledger as time and resources permit.

Sassano Italians who settled in Chicago from 1893 until the late 1950s lived on Federal Street (formerly Armour Avenue). Because the properties along 4700–5100 S Federal Street were purchased by the Chicago Housing Authority to build the Robert Taylor Homes, the PINs (Permanent Index Numbers) for that real estate no longer exist. This project is in the process of acquiring the Real Estate Tract Records and Warranty Deeds and transcribing the ledgers here.

How to read the tabs: Book–Page–Lot notation, e.g. B342-P328-L13-14 refers to Book 342, Page 328, Lots 13–14.

Only transactions involving Sassanesi Italians who owned real estate here have been transcribed — other individuals' transactions are left untranscribed. Where a page includes a hyperlinked page number, it leads to the original Torrens document; hyperlinks in the "Document" column lead to the actual document referenced.
